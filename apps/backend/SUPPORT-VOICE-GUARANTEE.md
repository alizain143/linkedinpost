# Support SOP: 7-day voice guarantee

Manual fulfillment for the first Pro or Agency subscription charge.

## Eligibility

1. First paid subscription charge for Pro or Agency (not renewals after the first period).
2. Request received within **7 days** of that charge.
3. Customer created a content profile and used generation (draft / Council / calendar / Autopilot).
4. Complaint is that drafts still feel generic / not in their voice.
5. **Not covered:** credit top-ups alone, multi-account abuse, obvious refund farming.

## Process

1. Confirm user email + Lemon invoice / order for the first paid charge.
2. Confirm profile exists and at least one AI generation occurred.
3. Refund the **first subscription charge** in Lemon Squeezy dashboard.
4. Confirm webhook downgrades plan (or manually verify user returns to Free).
5. Reply from **support@linkedinpost.ai** confirming refund timeline.

## Notes

- Do not auto-refund from the app in v1.
- Starter is legacy-only; new checkouts are Pro / Agency only.
- Ops: keep Lemon Starter variant for renewals; do not link it on pricing.
